It's still live poker

Everyone sits at one table, and the app never matches you with strangers. Your phone shows your two cards and, when it's your turn, the betting buttons. You peek at the cards by bending the corner, like real ones, and tap Check or Raise.

The chips are yours to push. Poker Dealer doesn't count them and has no idea how much anyone bet. Want the whole table to see the board? Put it on a tablet, a laptop or the TV in the middle of the table.

First poker night? Turn on beginner mode

Beginner mode gives you hints for the spot you're in and keeps a hand-rankings cheat sheet open whenever you need it. Nobody has to google whether a flush beats a straight in the middle of a hand. New to the game entirely? Start with the How to play intro.

When a hand goes all-in and the rest of the board is dealt out, everyone sees each player's chances to win. At showdown, the winner screen shows who won the hand. At the end of the night, there are stats.

FAQ

Do I need Telegram?

No. Poker Dealer is a Telegram Mini App, but it also runs in a browser: open in your browser. A friend without Telegram opens the invite link or scans the QR and types a name.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Poker Dealer is free.

How many people can play?

Two to six, all at one table.

Do we need chips?

Yes, bring your own. Poker Dealer only replaces the deck: it doesn't count chips and doesn't know the size of any bet.

Can you play for money in the app?

No. Poker Dealer doesn't handle money and doesn't count chips. It deals the cards and keeps track of whose turn it is.

Do I have to download something?

No. It's not in the App Store or Google Play — it opens right in Telegram or in a browser.
